Instrucciones de ingreso

1

Entrada al condominio

Al llegar, entrá por la caseta de seguridad del condominio. Si venís en coche, registrá tu vehículo en la caseta antes de estacionarte (los cajones no están asignados).

2

Cruzá hacia Coral

Cruzá de frente el edificio de Paseo del Sol y pasá el puente que conecta hacia Coral.

3

Subí al piso 3

Ingresá al elevador y subí al piso 3.

Acceso al departamento 305

Check-in instructions

1

Condominium entrance

Upon arrival, enter through the condominium security booth. If you're coming by car, please register your vehicle at the booth before parking (spaces are not assigned).

2

Cross over to Coral

Walk straight through the Paseo del Sol building and cross the bridge that connects to Coral.

3

Head up to the 3rd floor

Take the elevator and go up to the 3rd floor.

4

Access to apartment 305

Upon exiting, turn left, then left again, and walk to the back: there you'll find your apartment. It has a smart lock.
